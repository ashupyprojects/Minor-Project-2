💼 Employment Schemes

MGNREGA

MGNREGA guarantees up to 100 days of paid unskilled manual work each year for every rural household in India. It was enacted in 2005 and aims to strengthen rural livelihoods, create durable assets, reduce distress migration, and improve rural infrastructure. The scheme operates through Gram Panchayats, where job cards are issued and work is allotted within 15 days of demand. Wage payments are mandated within 15 days of work completion and are often paid via bank or post office accounts. Social audits and transparency mechanisms are built in to ensure accountability and timely delivery of benefits.

Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojana (PMKVY)

PMKVY is India’s flagship skill-certification scheme under the “Skill India” mission. Launched in 2015, it provides short-term training and recognition of prior learning to help youth gain industry-relevant skills and secure employment. Training is free for eligible beneficiaries, and certified candidates can access job support, placement assistance, or further specialization courses. The scheme is implemented through accredited training centres and monitored by the National Skills Development Corporation (NSDC). Its objective is to mobilize large numbers of Indian youth into gainful employment and improve productivity in sectors facing skill gaps.

Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)

DDU-GKY is a rural youth placement-linked skill training scheme under the Ministry of Rural Development. Launched in 2014, it aims to provide quality training and job placements for youth aged 15-35 from poor rural families. The scheme focuses on demand-driven courses aligned with industry needs and supports wage employment or self-employment. Training is delivered via empanelled training centres, and students receive skill certification, placement assistance, and follow-up support. The goal is to raise income levels and diversify livelihood opportunities in rural India.

Atal Innovation Mission (AIM)

AIM is the flagship innovation and entrepreneurship initiative of the Government of India, launched under NITI Aayog. It seeks to cultivate a culture of innovation across schools, colleges, research institutes, and MSMEs. Through Atal Tinkering Labs (ATLs), Atal New India Challenges, and innovation hubs, AIM nurtures problem-solving mindsets and supports start-ups. The mission’s aim is to position India among the world’s most innovative countries by fostering creativity, digital skills, and a supportive ecosystem for emerging technologies and enterprises.

PM SVANidhi Scheme

PM SVANidhi is a micro-credit scheme launched in June 2020 to support street vendors impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and strengthen their livelihoods. Under the scheme, eligible vendors receive a collateral-free working capital loan up to ₹10,000 in the first tranche, with additional loans and digital transaction incentives in subsequent tranches. The scheme also offers interest subsidy and credit-guarantee support. It aims to promote formalisation of street vending, digital payments, and friendly financial access for urban informal sector vendors.

Start-Up India & Stand-Up India

Start-Up India is a national initiative launched in 2016 to enable start-ups through simplifying regulatory procedures, offering tax incentives, incubators, and funding support. It provides a unified platform for entrepreneurs to register, access compliance relaxation, and connect to investors and mentors. Stand-Up India complements this by specifically facilitating bank loans between ₹10 lakh and ₹1 crore for at least one SC/ST borrower and one woman borrower per bank branch to set up a greenfield enterprise. Together, these schemes aim to create an inclusive entrepreneurial ecosystem, boost job creation, and accelerate economic growth in India.
