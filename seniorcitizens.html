👵 Senior Citizens Schemes

Pradhan Mantri Vaya Vandana Yojana (PMVVY)

The PMVVY scheme is a government-guaranteed pension plan for senior citizens aged 60 years and above. Launched by the Government of India, it aims to provide a steady income stream during old age by offering monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual pension payments. The scheme is administered through the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) under a government guarantee. It covers pension payments for a defined term and refunds purchase price to nominee in case of death. PMVVY provides financial stability to elderly individuals in face of low interest-rates and helps secure post-retirement income.

Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

IGNOAPS is a social assistance pension initiative of the Government of India targeting senior citizens from economically weaker sections. It provides monthly pension benefits to individuals aged 60 and above who cannot access other pension schemes and belong to rural or semi-urban settings. The objective is to reduce poverty and provide old-age income security to vulnerable elders, especially in situations where they have no alternative means of livelihood. Funding is shared between central and state governments and ensures dignity and support for aged people in minority or marginalised communities.

Varistha Pension Bima Yojana

The Varishtha Pension Bima Yojana is an immediate-annuity scheme launched by the Government of India for senior citizens (60 years and above) to provide guaranteed lifetime pension in return for a lump sum purchase price. Managed by LIC, it allowed pensioners to choose payment frequency (monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, annual). The scheme aimed to shield seniors from falling interest rates and provide stable post-retirement income. Pension amounts varied by frequency and premium amount; on death of the pensioner, the purchase price was refunded to nominee. It represents one of the key old-age income support programmes for elder citizens.

Senior Citizens Welfare Fund

The Senior Citizens Welfare Fund is a dedicated fund created by the Government of India to support welfare projects, health care and old-age services for senior citizens across the country. Contributions to the fund typically come from unclaimed amounts under the RBI/­government, and it is used to finance initiatives like mobile health vans, geriatric care at hospitals, awareness programmes and community support systems. The fund emphasises improving quality of life, medical coverage and social engagement of elders who lack adequate financial security or family support.

Ayushman Bharat (Elderly Coverage)

This flagship health-insurance scheme of the Government of India covers over 10 + crore vulnerable families, offering up to ₹5 lakh annual cover for secondary and tertiary hospitalization. For the elderly, this scheme provides crucial protection against catastrophic health expenses and helps families manage the rising cost of admission, medication and post-treatment care. With no restrictions on family size, age or gender, it ensures that older beneficiaries get equal access to empanelled hospitals, cashless treatment and comprehensive health care support. It greatly enhances financial security of older persons facing major health events.
