Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan

Samagra Shiksha Abhiyan is an integrated education program that combines Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan, Rashtriya Madhyamik Shiksha Abhiyan, and Teacher Education. Launched in 2018, it aims to ensure inclusive and equitable quality education from pre-school to Class 12. The scheme emphasizes improving learning outcomes, teacher training, digital classrooms, and infrastructure. It supports initiatives like NIPUN Bharat for foundational literacy and NEP 2020 goals. Funding is shared between the Centre and States, focusing on gender equality and inclusive education.

Mid-Day Meal Scheme

The Mid-Day Meal Scheme, now PM POSHAN, provides free nutritious meals to students of Classes I–VIII in government and aided schools. Started in 1995, it aims to enhance enrolment, attendance, and nutrition among children. The program ensures balanced meals prepared by local cooks or self-help groups and is monitored by school committees. It reduces classroom hunger and supports overall child development.

National Means-cum-Merit Scholarship (NMMS)

NMMS encourages academically talented students from economically weaker families to continue education beyond Class VIII. Launched in 2008, it provides ₹12,000 per year to selected students of Classes IX–XII whose parental income is below ₹3.5 lakh annually. Selection is based on a state-level examination. The scheme helps reduce dropouts and promotes secondary education among deserving students.

PM Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)

PMSS supports higher and technical education for children of ex-servicemen, ex-Coast Guard, and police personnel who lost lives in service. Managed by the Kendriya Sainik Board, it provides annual scholarships up to ₹30,000 for professional and degree courses. The scheme promotes education and welfare of defence families and is implemented through online registration.

AICTE Pragati Scholarship

The AICTE Pragati Scholarship empowers girl students pursuing technical education. It provides ₹50,000 annually for tuition and related expenses. Eligible candidates must be admitted in AICTE-approved institutions with a family income below ₹8 lakh per year. Up to two daughters per family can benefit. The scheme promotes women’s participation in STEM education.

SWAYAM

SWAYAM (Study Webs of Active-Learning for Young Aspiring Minds) is India’s massive open online course (MOOC) platform providing free quality education from school to university level. It offers courses from reputed institutions like IITs and IGNOU, with certification upon passing exams. The initiative promotes digital learning and lifelong education.
