National Scholarship Portal (NSP)

NSP is India's unified online portal for students to apply, track, and receive government scholarships at pre-matric, post-matric and higher education levels. Launched under the National e-Governance Plan, it simplifies registration, verification and disbursement of funds. With a one-time registration (OTR), students can apply to multiple schemes via a single dashboard, reducing paperwork and delays. It supports transparency, monitoring, and timely payments directly into bank accounts. By digitising the scholarship flow, NSP aims to boost student enrolments, reduce dropouts, and ensure that financial assistance reaches the intended beneficiaries efficiently.

Inspire Scholarship

The INSPIRE (Innovation in Science Pursuit for Inspired Research) Scholarship programme, implemented by the Department of Science & Technology, encourages outstanding young students to pursue studies and careers in natural sciences. It offers scholarships for higher education (such as in BSc/MSc) and mentorship to cultivate innovation and research skills. The scheme selects meritorious students (top 1% in board exams) and awards financial support annually, encouraging scientific talent and strengthening India's research base. By linking early schooling with advanced science education, INSPIRE fosters a culture of research-driven learning among youth.

PM YASASVI Scheme

PM YASASVI (“Young Achievers Scholarship Award Scheme for Vibrant India”) targets OBC, EBC and DNT students to access top class education in school and college. The scheme offers scholarships and support to meritorious students from under-represented communities, enabling them to study at schools/colleges of excellence. It intends to enhance equity in education, reduce marginalisation and enable higher educational achievement among these groups. By providing financial help, mentoring and academic pathways, YASASVI opens doors to quality learning for youth who may otherwise face systemic barriers to advanced educational opportunities.

AICTE Pragati & Saksham

The AICTE Pragati & Saksham Scholarship Schemes support female students and specially-abled students enrolling in Diploma/Degree technical programmes in AICTE-approved institutions. Pragati focuses on girl students (income ceiling ₹8 lakh, up to two daughters/family), while Saksham targets differently-abled students. The scholarship covers tuition/education expenses (up to ₹50,000/year) for technical courses, easing financial burden and promoting diversity in STEM education. Together, they encourage inclusive participation in engineering, technology and allied fields, empowering students often under-represented in technical education environments.

Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme

The Post-Matric Scholarship Scheme provides financial assistance to students from Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST) and other disadvantaged categories who are pursuing studies beyond matriculation in India. It aims to increase enrolment, reduce drop-outs and improve representation of these students in higher education. The scheme offers maintenance allowance, tuition fee reimbursement and support for eligible courses based on community and income criteria. By extending help through post-secondary years, it facilitates better educational access and enhances employability of students from vulnerable backgrounds.
