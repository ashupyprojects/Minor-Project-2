Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP)

Launched on 22 January 2015, Beti Bachao Beti Padhao (BBBP) is a flagship initiative of the Government of India aimed at improving the Child Sex Ratio (CSR) and promoting the survival, protection, education and empowerment of the girl child. The tri-ministerial scheme (Women & Child Development, Health & Family Welfare, Education) focuses on multi-sectoral action in selected districts, community mobilisation, awareness campaigns, enforcing the PC & PNDT Act and supporting girls' education. It calls for societal mindset changes, encourages public participation and aims to ensure that every girl child is celebrated and educated.

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY)

Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana (PMMVY) is a maternity benefit programme launched on 1 January 2017 by the Ministry of Women & Child Development. It provides cash incentives to pregnant and lactating women (for the first living child, and later expanded) to compensate for wage loss, encourage early registration of pregnancy, antenatal & postnatal check-ups, institutional delivery, and promote good nutrition practices. The scheme aims to improve maternal and child health outcomes, reduce dropout of pregnant women from health services and support safe childbirth practices across India.

One Stop Centre Scheme (Sakhi Centres)

The One Stop Centre Scheme, popularly known as Sakhi Centres, was launched in 2015 by the Ministry of Women & Child Development to support women affected by violence, both in private and public spaces. These centres provide integrated services such as medical aid, police assistance, legal counselling, psycho-social support, and temporary shelter under one roof. Available 24x7 in every district, the centres also facilitate video-conferencing for court hearings and link survivors to existing government programmes. The initiative strengthens institutional response to gender-based violence, ensuring safety, dignity, and justice for women in distress.

Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS)

The Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS) scheme, launched in 1975, is a flagship programme of Government of India functioning through Anganwadi centres to provide early childhood care, preschool education, supplementary nutrition, immunisation, health check-ups and referral services for children under 6 years and pregnant/lactating mothers. It aims to improve nutritional and health status, enhance development and learning readiness of children, reduce dropout rates, and strengthen collaboration between Women & Child Development and Health ministries. ICDS ensures that foundational years are supported through integrated services and strong grassroots outreach.

POSHAN Abhiyaan

POSHAN Abhiyaan (National Nutrition Mission) was launched by the Government of India on 8 March 2018 to improve nutritional outcomes for children (0-6 years), adolescent girls, pregnant women and lactating mothers. It aims to reduce stunting, under-nutrition, anaemia and low birth weight through a convergent approach, community engagement (“Jan Andolan”), technology-enabled monitoring, growth tracking and nutrition education. The scheme emphasises coordinated action across health, women & child development, agriculture and education sectors, strengthening the Anganwadi system, and promoting nutritional awareness at community level.

Working Women Hostel Scheme

Working Women Hostel Scheme aims to provide safe, affordable accommodation for working women, especially from rural, backward or urban migrant backgrounds. The scheme promotes employment and mobility by supporting women to live near workplaces, reducing commute burdens and enhancing security. Hostels are set up under Women & Child Development ministry subsidised by the government with supportive infrastructure, enabling women to pursue jobs, training and entrepreneurial activities without relocation or safety concerns. The scheme contributes to women's empowerment, economic participation, and bridging gender-based barriers to employment.
