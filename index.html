Frequently Asked Questions

Checkout our knowledge base for some of your answers!

What is Bharat Portal?
Bharat Portal is a platform which shows Government schemes and services at single place. You can find schemes, check eligibility, and apply.
How will Bharat Portal help common citizens?
It lets citizens discover and apply for central/state schemes from one place—no multiple websites needed.
Can I apply for schemes through Bharat portal?
Yes. Many schemes support direct application or take you to the official portal to apply.
How do I check my eligibility?
Fill the eligibility form; recommendations are shown based on your profile and inputs.
What information about a scheme can I find?
Eligibility, benefits, required documents, process, timelines, and official links.
