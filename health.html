🏥 Health Schemes

Ayushman Bharat - PM Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY)

This flagship health insurance scheme provides an annual cover of ₹5 lakh per family for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization in empanelled public and private hospitals. It targets vulnerable families (~12 crore households) identified through SECC-2011 data, with no restriction on family size, age, or gender. Coverage includes pre-hospitalization (3 days) and post-hospitalization (15 days) costs, plus medicine and diagnostic expenses. Fully government-funded, it aims to reduce catastrophic health expenditure and improve access to quality care across India.

Mission Indradhanush

Launched in December 2014, this immunization programme aims to rapidly enhance full vaccination coverage among children and pregnant women. It focuses on “left-out” and “dropped-out” individuals under the Universal Immunization Programme, delivering vaccines for multiple preventable diseases in high-risk districts. The scheme deploys special campaigns and outreach, strengthening health systems and monitoring to achieve over 90 % coverage, thereby reducing child mortality and disease burden.

Janani Suraksha Yojana (JSY)

JSY is a safe-motherhood initiative that promotes institutional deliveries through cash incentives to pregnant women, especially in rural and low-income areas. It aims to reduce maternal and neonatal mortality by encouraging skilled birth attendance and postnatal care. The scheme integrates with Janani Shishu Suraksha Karyakram (JSSK) for free services during pregnancy, childbirth, and postnatal period. It targets regions with low institutional delivery rates and emphasizes inclusion of scheduled caste/tribe and other vulnerable groups.

Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)

PMSMA is an antenatal care programme that offers free check-ups and high-risk pregnancy screening on the 9th of each month across the country. Aimed at reducing maternal morbidity and mortality, it encourages women in their second trimester of pregnancy to visit public health facilities for assured care. It promotes early detection of complications, including gestational diabetes and hypertension, and links pregnant women with referral systems ensuring timely and safe delivery.

Rashtriya Swasthya Bima Yojana (RSBY)

RSBY was launched to provide health insurance coverage to the Below-Poverty-Line (BPL) population, covering inpatient treatment up to a specified sum for families. It aimed to protect vulnerable families from catastrophic health expenditures by enabling cashless hospitalization through empanelled hospitals. Over time many of RSBY’s functions have been subsumed under PMJAY to provide broader and deeper coverage.

National Digital Health Mission (NDHM)

NDHM (also called Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission) aims to build the digital backbone for India’s healthcare ecosystem by issuing Health IDs, managing electronic health records, and maintaining registries of professionals and facilities. It facilitates interoperable data exchange, digital access to services, and seamless care pathways. The mission strengthens telemedicine, personalised health tracking, and broader access to health infrastructure through digital means, thereby promoting Universal Health Coverage and efficient healthcare delivery.
